  content is one-to-one chat tied to a single order, plus star ratings with
  optional text. Every conversation has a flag icon in the top bar. It opens a
  sheet with report reasons and, at the bottom, "Bloquear esta pessoa" (Block
  this person). Blocking hides that person's messages and prevents writing to
  them; a bar appears in the conversation with an Unblock button. Reports open
  our in-app support conversation with the report pre-written, which our team
  reads and answers. Ratings with free text are moderated from our admin panel
  and can be hidden.

- Paid content or features. The app sells no digital content and uses no
  in-app purchase. Everything paid is a physical good or a service consumed
  outside the app: grocery and restaurant delivery, cleaning, car wash,
  bookings and rides. Payment methods are card (Stripe), MB WAY and cash. For
  testing please choose "Dinheiro" (cash) at checkout, because card and MB WAY

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Bora is a local delivery and services marketplace for the city of Guarda,
Portugal, a city of about 40,000 people.

The problem it solves: national delivery apps concentrate on large cities. In
Guarda, ordering from a supermarket, booking a barber or getting an errand
done still means phone calls and guesswork. Bora puts the city's shops,
restaurants, pharmacies and service providers in one place, with the price
visible before ordering and live tracking afterwards.

The value it provides: one app for groceries, pharmacy, restaurants, barber
and beauty bookings, home cleaning, errands, car wash, table reservations and
ride-hailing, delivered by couriers who know the city.

Target audience: adults living in and around Guarda. One app serves three
roles - customer, courier and partner store.

3. SETTING UP AND ACCESSING THE MAIN FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
No setup is required. Browsing works without an account.

Demo accounts:
  Customer (browse and order):  demo@bora.app        / BoraDemo2026!
  Account deletion test only:   demo.apagar@bora.app / BoraDemo2026!

The second account exists so that testing deletion does not lock you out of
the first one. It is recreated automatically every hour, so it is always
available even if you delete it more than once.

Steps:
  1. Launch the app. A privacy and cookies sheet appears with three buttons:
     "Aceitar tudo" (Accept all), "Rejeitar" (Reject) and "Gerir preferencias"
     (Manage preferences). Any of the three continues; nothing in the app
     depends on consent being granted.
  2. Choose "Sou Cliente" (I am a customer) and sign in with the demo account.
  3. The demo account already has a saved address in Guarda, so stores load
     without GPS and from any country. If the store list looks empty, open the
     address selector at the top of the home screen and pick the saved
     address.
  4. To place a test order, choose "Dinheiro" (cash) at checkout.

Orders placed from the demo accounts are sandboxed: a database trigger marks
them as test orders and assigns them to a demo courier that is offline, so
they are never dispatched to a real courier.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The app is offered in Portugal and Brazil. There are no region-gated features:
the app behaves identically in both, and no content is shown in one region and
hidden in another.

To be completely transparent: delivery operations currently run only in
Guarda, Portugal. Someone outside that delivery area can browse, create an
account and use the app, but will see no stores available for their address.
The interface is in Portuguese, with an English toggle inside the app.

6. REGULATED ACTIVITIES AND PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Ride-hailing (TVDE). The app includes passenger ride-hailing, shown as "Bora
Motorista" on the home screen. In Portugal this activity is regulated by
Decree-Law 45/2018. Drivers who accept passenger rides are onboarded
separately from delivery couriers and must hold a valid TVDE driver
certificate and drive a licensed TVDE vehicle. We collect and check those
documents before a driver can go online for rides, and a driver without
approved documents cannot receive one. Passengers need no documents. We are
happy to provide the operator documentation if you need to see it.

Pharmacy. We deliver over-the-counter pharmacy and parapharmacy products from
a licensed pharmacy in Guarda. We do not sell prescription medicines, and the
app gives no diagnosis and no medical advice.

Alcohol. The supermarket catalogues we deliver from include alcoholic drinks,
the same way a supermarket shelf does. The app does not encourage consumption
and has no alcohol-themed content. We sell no tobacco: we checked the entire
catalogue and the only nicotine item is a smoking-cessation gum sold by the
licensed pharmacy.

Third-party material. Product names and photos come from the stores the order
is collected from, and store names are used descriptively to say where an
order is collected. Partner stores have signed agreements with us. This is why
we declared "uses third-party content" in App Store Connect.
