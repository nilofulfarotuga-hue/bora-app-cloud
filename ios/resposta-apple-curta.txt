2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Bora is a local delivery and services marketplace for Guarda, Portugal (about
40,000 people): groceries, pharmacy, restaurants, barber and beauty bookings,
home cleaning, errands, car wash, reservations and ride-hailing. National apps
concentrate on large cities; here, ordering still means phone calls. Audience:
adults in and around Guarda. One app, three roles: customer, courier, partner.

3. ACCESS
Browsing works without an account.
  demo@bora.app        / BoraDemo2026!  (browse and order; please do not delete)
  demo.apagar@bora.app / BoraDemo2026!  (deletion test; recreated hourly)
A privacy sheet appears first; any of its three buttons continues. Tap "Sou
Cliente" and sign in. The demo account has a saved Guarda address, so stores
load from any country. Demo orders are sandboxed and never reach a real courier.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase (database, authentication, realtime, storage, server functions),
Stripe (card and MB WAY), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push), Google Maps SDK,
Directions and Places. No AI services, no ad networks, no third-party analytics.

5. REGIONS
Offered in Portugal and Brazil, identical behaviour, nothing region-gated.
Delivery runs only in Guarda: outside it you can browse and sign up but will
see no stores. Portuguese interface with an English toggle inside the app.

6. REGULATED ACTIVITIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Ride-hailing is regulated in Portugal by Decree-Law 45/2018. Ride drivers are
onboarded separately and must hold a valid TVDE certificate and a licensed
vehicle; we check those documents before they can go online. Passengers need
none, and we can provide operator documentation on request. Pharmacy: over-the-
counter products from a licensed pharmacy, no prescription medicines and no
medical advice. Alcohol appears in supermarket catalogues as on any shelf; we
sell no tobacco. Product names and photos come from the stores an order is
collected from, which is why we declared "uses third-party content".
